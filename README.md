# Zepto-Sales-Dashboard(Interactive Dashboard created using MS Excel)

## PROJECT OBJECTIVES

- Analyze Zepto's overall sales performance.
- Monitor key business KPIs.
- Analyze sales trends across months and days.
- Compare sales across different customer segments.
- Analyze customer types based on quantity purchased.
- Compare orders based on payment methods.
- Analyze customers based on their cities.
- Identify top-performing orders and product categories.
- Understand sales contribution across different product categories.

- <a href="https://github.com/Amrusha25/ZEPTO-SALES-DASHBOARD/blob/main/ZEPTO%20SALES%20DASHBOARD.xlsx">Dataset</a>

## Questions (KPIs)

- What is the total sales?

- What is the total number of orders?

- What is the total number of customers?

- What is the total number of products?

- What is the total quantity sold?

- What is the average sales value?

- Identify the top 10 orders based on sales.

- Analyze the trend of sales over months.

- Analyze the volume of sales over days.

- Compare sales across different customer segments.

- Analyze the contribution of customer types to quantity sold.

- Analyze the contribution of orders by payment method.

- Analyze the contribution of customers by city.

- Compare sales across different product categories.

- Analyze the contribution of each product category to total sales.
